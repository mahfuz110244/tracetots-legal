TraceTots — Privacy policy

A tracing app for young children

Last updated: 22 September 2026

TraceTots is a tracing app for young children. This policy explains what information the app keeps and how it is protected. In short: everything stays on this device.

What the app keeps

To work, the app saves the following on this device only: the child's first name, age group and learning languages; tracing progress, stars and stickers; app settings such as sound and app language; and, if a parent adds one, a profile picture.

What the app does not collect

The app does not ask for an email address, last name or date of birth, and has no accounts. It does not use location, contacts, the camera or the microphone. The app has no internet access, so none of this information ever leaves the device.

Profile picture

A profile picture is optional. It is chosen with the Android photo picker, so the app only receives the one image that is picked, not the rest of the photo library. A small square copy is saved in the app's private storage. It is never uploaded or shared, and it can be removed at any time by tapping the picture.

No ads, no tracking

The app shows no advertising and contains no third-party analytics or tracking tools.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any information. The app's data is also excluded from device backups and device-to-device transfers.

Deleting information

To erase a child's information, open Parent dashboard → Settings → Delete this child's data. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from the device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear on this page and in the app with a new date.
